### 🧹 Cleanup

- Reconciliation, initial construction and migration lower proposed field facts
  through the existing accepted-catalog authority. Callers explicitly retain
  identity, physical slot, introduced layout and historical fill. Later migration
  stages use the snapshot's existing updates instead of rebuilding unchanged
  components. Accepted snapshots remain runtime authority.
- Journal record emission and allocation-free sizing share one exhaustive layout.
  Byte output retains checked little-endian length prefixes; counting saturates
  on overflow. Independent bounded decoding, semantic admission, fingerprints,
  format identities and corruption checks remain intact. A fixed-byte control
  guards the current schema-publication record layout.
- Facade category/code and origin projections use local declaration macros.
  Public enum identities, variants, named Debug, numeric codes, cause mapping and
  Wire envelopes remain unchanged; compact diagnostic types retain their own
  formatting. Core origins reuse their existing diagnostic projection.
- Unique and row-local constraint validation share forward/verify progression,
  revision fences and terminal handling. Their compiled proofs, scan payloads,
  forward publishers and promotion functions remain kind-specific. Unique pages
  publish staged entries and job progress atomically through their existing owner.
- Scalar scan finalization consumes the existing prepared runtime bundle and
  applies terminal/continuation choices plus exact-probe overrides in one place.
  Execution consumes that finalized route without later hint mutation. Logical
  windows, physical fetch, load budgets, lookahead and index-range specifications
  keep their distinct semantics; covering and aggregate specializations remain.
- Migration tests share the exact store-local cold-restart sequence for field
  removal, identity removal and entity rename, including nested SQL lineage
  callers. Durable source data, interruption points and scenario assertions remain
  in their original owners. No generalized fixture framework is introduced.

The six cleanups pass 705 focused cases (671 engine and 34 facade diagnostics)
on Rust 1.99.1, strict affected library/test-target Clippy with all features,
and strict default-feature library Clippy. Formatting and diff whitespace checks
pass. Full workspace/release, native macOS and installed Canic qualification are
not part of this development gate; IC/Wasm cost changes remain unmeasured.
