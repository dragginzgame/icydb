# 0.263 — Seek Integration Qualification

Authorized 2026-10-01 after the scoped-ready 0.262 closeout and completion of
its two remaining consolidation findings. Delivery owner: [status](0.263-status.md).

## Outcome and demonstrated gap

Determine whether primary-key positioning materially reduces the cost of live
sparse ordered intersections, then complete the justified production integration
or retire the unused prototype. Successful results, ordering, page unions,
cursor resume, accepted-index integrity and execution budgets remain maintained.
The outcome concerns query execution; schema migration, entity creation and
write folding are outside this line.

At kickoff, the physical primary and index leaves carried a held-head
seek/consume protocol. Its prefix adapter had no pending-target writer; ordinary
merge/intersection owners polled through OrderedKeyStream. S1 found no qualified
end-to-end benefit, so S2 retired that prototype. Physical leaves and composites
now expose the maintained polling contract; authenticated cursor binding remains
independently live. See the S2 decision below.

This gap existed in the introducing 0.222.0 commit, `728dfa8d4`; it is unfinished
integration rather than evidence of a previously connected route being removed.
Historical descriptions of a proved cursor-bearing seek route exceed the
released call graph. Do not use those descriptions or historical cost samples
as current qualification.

## Current owners and route boundaries

- Accepted-root prefix cardinalities and existing traversal admission own
  eligibility. Exact-prefix probes admit two or three children with a total
  cardinality no greater than 256; missing, stale or oversized proofs retain
  the existing conservative decision. This line does not increase those bounds.
- Cursorless admitted intersections can use IndexScan's direct structural
  probe. It exhausts complete prefixes, validates Present row witnesses and
  compares physical counts with synchronized cardinality authority.
- A continuation boundary excludes that direct probe. The traversal owner
  constructs physically ordered child streams and collects their overlap
  through ordinary polling. Other non-applicable intersection shapes also
  retain ordinary stream behavior.
- Physical access owns raw bounds, index suffix encoding, refill/decode and
  existing cursor-anchor binding. Composite streams own alignment and output
  deduplication; page and budget owners retain admission and failure accounting.

Measure resumed polling first and keep the cursorless direct probe as a control.
The existing SQL-perf actor, generated fixture declarations and focused IC test
owner are the preferred qualification surface. Extend those owners only where
the maintained fixture cannot represent the necessary sparse distribution.
Do not restore the retired frozen 0.222 harness or introduce a parallel canister.

## No-build decision and implementation constraints

The demonstrated maintenance gap is an unused production protocol. A query-cost
benefit remains unmeasured. Deleting the prototype while retaining ordinary
polling is a valid outcome if a candidate does not justify its ongoing cost.
Do not enable seeking simply because its unit tests pass or its name promises it.

Compare the simplest changes in the existing owners: reduce resumed probe
overhead, or position eligible ordered physical children during alignment.
Reusing structural probing must preserve its complete-prefix integrity proof;
binding a smaller cursor range does not prove that the full prefix count matches
its accepted authority. Keep the direct probe unchanged unless an equivalent
integrity boundary is demonstrated.

For any seek candidate, charge completed work accurately and admit each
indivisible storage operation before changing the held key or physical bounds.
Budget failure, corruption, page interruption and exhaustion stay distinct.
ASC/DESC comparisons, duplicate physical occurrences and equal/behind targets
must preserve exactly-once consumption. Existing primary and prefix cursor
bindings remain authoritative; no cursor translator or predecessor format is
permitted. Never save divergent child heads into a scalar cursor by inference.

Prefer one capability in the existing physical/composite owner over a new stream
hierarchy, mode or independent dispatcher. Carry planner-proven eligibility
forward; do not rediscover it from SQL text or generated models at runtime.
Transparent observers and budget wrappers must retain their accounting. A
production caller must actually supply a target and consume the resulting head;
another inert adapter or exclusively direct unit-test caller is insufficient.

Initial state-space delta is zero: this design changes no runtime route. Before
implementation, record the candidate's demonstrated benefit, simplest alternative,
canonical owner and any added execution combinations. Avoid configuration,
persisted seek state, new cursor formats or public API changes. Persisted and
protocol boundaries remain current version 1. Narrowing or retiring unused
internal surfaces is a hard cut.

## Qualification and decision evidence

S1 freezes query identities, data, expected rows and the actual selected route.
The [S1 baseline](s1-baseline.md) records a demonstrated frontend boundary:
signed dynamic filters retain numeric-widening admission and scan the fixture,
while SQL strict predicates select intersections and unsigned native dynamic
filters qualify the intersection path. SQL EXPLAIN cannot stand in for the
dynamic page's selected route. An IC resumed-intersection comparison remains
unqualified by that signed actor; preserve this limit in the S2 decision.
Cover two/three exact prefixes, ASC/DESC, sparse separated heads, empty overlap,
overlap near the end and denser controls within maintained admission. Include
cursorless and resumed execution, small pages and residual filtering where the
current planner admits them. Report unsupported or non-selected shapes explicitly;
no planner widening is implied by the matrix.

Maintain focused correctness evidence for ordering, page termination, every
issued cursor's resume suffix, total limits and exactly-once output. Use live
query/session boundaries alongside the physical fixtures. Retain corruption
and hard-budget counterexamples at the relevant production boundary. S2 must
demonstrate a reachable target writer and consumer if seeking is selected.

Use matched frozen raw wasm-release artifacts with identical features,
post-link options, data and setup. Collect IC instruction counts and whole-call
charged cycles separately, keeping startup, fixture writes, folding and deferred
charges outside the measured windows. Report raw Wasm bytes as the primary size
metric. Native or wall-clock timings are not performance evidence.

Record sparse savings, control regressions and implementation complexity together.
Avoid inherited historical ratio gates or unmeasured performance promises.
Accept integration only when repeatable end-to-end evidence justifies its cost
without weakening the maintained contracts. Otherwise retain polling and remove
the unused subsystem and its exclusively prototype tests. Port useful bounds,
pagination and corruption assertions to maintained calls.

## Delivery boundary

The [three planned landings](0.263-status.md) separate qualification tooling,
the production decision and final IC breadth qualification. Each includes its

Mainnet query costs and MMO sustained write throughput are not established by

## S2 decision — retain polling and retire the prototype

S1 does not qualify a resumed admitted-intersection IC benefit. The signed IC
actor scans, while the maintained cursorless admitted route validates complete
prefixes. Integration would require new alignment state and accounting without
a measured benefit, and seeking cannot replace that full-prefix integrity proof.
The smallest justified outcome is retirement of the unused seek protocol.

The physical owner retains bounded polling, authenticated primary resume and
index anchors. Traversal retains existing admission and complete-prefix probing;
composite owners retain ordinary alignment and duplicate handling. Remove the
pending-target adapter, its dispatch variant, held-key fields, repositioning
methods and seek-only accounting/tests. Port useful suffix, bounds, duplicate,
corruption and hard-budget assertions to maintained polling calls. No planner
widening, configuration, cursor format or public surface is added. State-space
shrinks by one internal stream variant and the dormant held-head protocol.

S2 compares bounded sparse/rotated controls on matched frozen S1 and candidate
artifacts. S3 remains the separate final full-matrix qualification. An eventual
seek proposal needs a demonstrated admitted IC workload and repeatable savings;
schema-bound signed lookup admission remains an independent follow-up.

## Final disposition — S3 complete

The [closeout](s3-closeout.md) reports scoped-ready completion of all three
landings, full matched IC receipts and the final call-graph audit. Ordinary
polling remains the maintained contract; the unused seek prototype is retired.
No admitted resumed-intersection IC benefit or uniform speedup is established.
The signed lookup admission follow-up is outside this completed line; release
validation and publication remain user-owned.
