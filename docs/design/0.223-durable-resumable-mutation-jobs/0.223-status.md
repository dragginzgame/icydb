## Design Reconciliation

The original design was cut against `0.222.2`. The implementation baseline is
the final published `0.222.4` tree because closeout corrections changed the
real predecessor's executor and Wasm shape. The durable mutation architecture
is otherwise unchanged.

Patch 1 confirms four distinct relationships:

- reuse the existing bounded Forward/Verify engine and aggregate request root;
- extend the excluded progress allocation with a separate mutation key/record;
- extend the current marker with one atomic progress replacement effect; and
- preserve generic revision-strict jobs unchanged rather than disabling their
  post-operation proof comparison.

The application-custodied prepare/resume API remains current only until Patch
5 lands its durable replacement. It will then be removed as a pre-1.0 hard cut,
without an alias or continuation importer.

## Patch 1 Evidence

- One machine-readable contract freezes the exact baseline tag/commit/tree,
  current owner markers, 10,001-row tier/scoring SQL, 256-key/64-update limits,
  record limits, nine failpoints, instruction ceilings, and Wasm limits.
- The current exact one-shot update ceiling is 4,096, below the 10,001-row
  incident fixture.
- An all-stale fixture requires at least 157 Forward calls; clean verification
  requires at least 40 calls. Completion cannot be inferred from one bounded
  page or one successful target commit.
- The current 512-row control reproduces at 430,996 prepare instructions,
  25.55M-27.19M per Forward call, and 6.43M-6.44M per Verify call.
- Mutation-job state, replay, acknowledgement, and stable-growth measurements
  begin in Patch 2 when their distinct record exists; Patch 1 fixes their names,
  measurement points, and ceilings instead of substituting generic-job costs.
- The published dynamic-query subject is 2,607,381 raw final Wasm bytes and
  1,021,818 deterministic gzip bytes. The typed-query subject is 1,792,657 raw
  bytes and 681,772 deterministic gzip bytes. Production Candid and method
  modes are unchanged.
- New mutation record and marker formats do not exist yet. Their exact encoded
  sizes are Patch 2 and Patch 4 acceptance evidence; Patch 1 freezes their
  maximums and does not misreport unavailable sizes as zero.

## Current Authorization

The next implementation handoff is Patch 2 only. It may add the distinct
mutation-job type family and bounded progress codec, state/replay, and terminal
acknowledgement. It may not start SQL custody, change the marker, mutate target
rows, relax generic proof jobs, or begin Forward execution.
