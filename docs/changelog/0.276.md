# 0.276 — License grants

## [0.276.0]

### ⚠️ Breaking

New material is offered under the Dragginz Source Available License — Verifiable
ICP Burn 1.0. See the [complete terms](../../LICENSE) and
[contributor acceptance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Third-party live deployments
must satisfy the license's mainnet burn and evidence requirements; section 5
sets out the Toko Studios Ltd operational exception. Contributors must expressly
accept that exception and have authority to offer their material on those terms.

All workspace packages inherit the license-file selection. Publishable crates
include the same terms and retain the original Apache-2.0 and MIT notices for
previously licensed material. Existing grants remain attached to the material
they cover; the notices do not offer later, exclusively Dragginz-licensed
contributions under those earlier licenses. Package versions are unchanged.
