# 0.233 Durable Mutation-Job Liveness And Recovery

## Closeout carried by 0.234.0

This work was implemented after `v0.233.2` but ships as part of `0.234.0`
instead of another 0.233 patch release. The detail remains here because it
closes the 0.233 mutation-job design line.

### ➕ Added

- Public mutation-job target failures now carry one bounded
  `MutationJobTargetFailureReason`: `StagingByteBudgetExceeded` for the exact
  writer-owned staging boundary and `Other` for every remaining target error.
- The nested metrics report exposes privacy-safe mutation-job lifecycle,
  restart, target-failure, committed-step, resource-total, and retained-capacity
  facts. It deliberately excludes job IDs, SQL, keys, values, replay keys, and
  continuation bytes.
- Progress inventory now includes the exact encoded byte total of all retained
  validated records alongside its existing count, reservation, family, and
  lifecycle projection.

### 🩹 Fixed

- An admitted mutation page that reaches the deterministic engine execution
  budget now commits terminal
  `RestartRequired(ExecutionBudgetPolicyExceeded)` progress instead of leaving
  the same Active checkpoint available for an infinite retry loop.
- Mutation-job advance now resolves its retained immutable entity source path
  through source-key authority rather than the editable SQL-name lookup, so
  authored source and display names may differ without a false schema restart.
- Mutation-job session operations now pass through their configured metrics
  sink, preserving exact replay and commit ordering while making successful
  starts, loads, advances, transitions, cancellation, acknowledgement, and
  inventory observable.

### 🔧 Changed

- Forward now examines at most 4,096 keys and stages at most 240 rows. The row
  limit is derived from canonical commit-work and maximum-index-fanout
  authority; Verify independently rises to 4,096 keys.
- Every non-replay advance uses a fixed 30-billion-instruction allocation with
  a 5-billion failure reserve and further margin below the IC update-message
  ceiling. The complete budget profile contributes an owner-derived component
  to the batch-policy identity.
- The final 0.233 mutation batch-policy identity is `0x195dc27a` and includes
  the execution-budget terminalization rule as well as the earlier managed-time,
  count, byte, checkpoint, and entity-revision rules. Retained jobs created
  under 0.233.2 terminate through `BatchPolicyChanged` and must be restarted.
- The test-admin audit surface adds an extended metrics query for real-canister
  evidence. Production omits that wrapper and retains the same 70 exports.

### ⚠️ Breaking

- `MutationJobError::TargetMutationFailed` now carries a bounded reason,
  `MutationJobRestartReason` gains `ExecutionBudgetPolicyExceeded`, progress
  inventory gains `retained_record_bytes`, and the nested public metrics DTO
  gains mutation-job facts. These are pre-1.0 exhaustive Rust/Candid shape
  changes; no predecessor alias, decoder, or fallback is retained.
- Persisted records remain the sole current version-1 form. The restart-reason
  encoding adds one current tag without adding a version or compatibility
  path; retained earlier-policy jobs use the existing terminal restart route.

### 📚 Documentation

- The public facade and SQL contract now agree on 4,096 Forward keys, 240
  Forward rows, 4,096 Verify keys, independent 16 MiB Forward scan and staging
  budgets, the 16 MiB Verify scan budget, fixed execution headroom, statement
  time versus per-message managed-write time, IcyDB-owned continuations, fresh
  single-incarnation IDs, exact cancellation limits, and 64/56/8 capacity
  arithmetic.
- The persisted-format inventory now records the current entity-revision
  authority, bounded predecessor initialization, affected-entity advancement,
  fail-closed validation, and absence of a legacy fallback.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native coverage closes every lifecycle, restart, and target-failure bucket;
  exact scan, staging, key, row, retained-byte, and capacity accounting; current
  payload/Candid round trips; fail-closed inventory; and final policy identity.
- Injected deterministic Forward and Verify exhaustion proves terminal progress,
  unchanged target rows, and exact receipt replay without re-execution.
- PocketIC proves Forward and Verify commitments, exact replay, unrelated-write
  convergence, target-revision restart, stable completion, terminal replay,
  acknowledgement, and privacy-safe metrics. The full repository validation
  workflow remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Relative to live `0.233.2`, compiler Wasm grows by 12,219 bytes to 5,051,508
  and final raw Wasm grows by 11,018 bytes to 4,412,796 (`+0.2503%`), within
  the 64 KiB patch review threshold. Deterministic gzip grows by 5,145 bytes to
  1,712,132; Candid grows by 436 bytes to 61,408; exports remain 70.
- The 512-row real-canister Forward proof uses 240 / 240 / 32-row pages at
  115,056,676 / 122,858,524 / 21,252,080 instructions. A complete 240-row page
  at 64 secondary indexes costs 4,948,825,024 under the 7.5-billion review
  ceiling; both 10,001-row upgrade fixtures complete in 42 Forward and three
  Verify calls.
- The 8,193-row real-canister lifecycle costs 110,347,590 instructions for the
  first 4,096-key Verify, 124,872 for exact replay, 107,516,596 after an
  unrelated write, 1,523,295 for target drift, and 110,338,161 / 107,492,238 /
  1,666,377 for stable completion. State, terminal replay, and acknowledgement
  cost 104,993 / 106,131 / 117,863.
- Full 64-slot inventory costs 5,880,547 instructions and reports 42,344 exact
  encoded retained-record bytes, below the existing 7,000,000 review ceiling.

## 0.233.2 - 2026-08-19

### 🩹 Fixed

- Durable mutation-job Verify now observes only the target entity's durable
  journal revision. Writes to another entity in the same physical store no
  longer cause artificial Forward restarts, while writes to the target before
  or during any Verify page still restart the proof.
- Forward-to-Verify persists the journal owner's exact resulting target
  revision for a mutating page, or its current target revision for a
  zero-update page. Every later Verify message compares that original baseline
  before and after scanning and carries it unchanged through intermediate
  checkpoints.

### 🔧 Changed

- Journaled stores retain one sorted, packed current version-1 entity-revision
  authority. Each row batch advances only the distinct affected entities;
  exact replay is idempotent, folding preserves the authority, and accepted
  schema publication preserves existing tags while initializing new tags from
  the conservative store-wide revision.
- Startup convergence initializes missing metadata for accepted predecessor
  state from the validated store-wide data revision without scanning rows.
  Once the completion marker exists, malformed chunks, missing entries, or an
  accepted-schema tag mismatch fail closed instead of falling back.

### 🧭 Migration Notes

- The mutation batch-policy identity changes for entity-scoped Verify.
  Retained jobs created under an earlier 0.233 landing terminate through the
  existing `BatchPolicyChanged` restart contract and must be restarted; no
  predecessor continuation executor is retained.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native coverage proves conservative initialization, exact reentry,
  malformed/missing authority rejection, affected-entity isolation,
  multi-entity mutation, replay, fold survival, cross-page baseline retention,
  and all four row/progress marker interruption boundaries.
- PocketIC writes an unrelated entity between Verify pages without a restart,
  then writes the target entity, observes exactly one restart, and completes
  stably. Focused warning-denied core, audit-canister, and integration checks
  pass; repository-wide validation remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Packed entity metadata uses 102 raw bytes for three entities and 65,617
  bytes across one control plus two data chunks at the accepted 4,096-entity
  limit. Both shapes remain within the journal allocation's existing nine
  64 KiB stable pages, for zero measured page growth.
- The first and unrelated-entity Verify pages cost 7,497,160 and 7,546,266
  instructions under the unchanged 8,000,000 ceiling. Target drift restarts in
  1,508,773 instructions; stable completion pages cost 7,505,156, 7,538,338,
  and 4,300,953 instructions.
- Relative to pushed `0.233.1`, compiler Wasm grows by 15,265 bytes to
  5,039,289 and final raw Wasm grows by 13,310 bytes to 4,401,778
  (`+0.303%`). Deterministic gzip grows by 6,174 bytes to 1,706,987; Candid
  grows by 125 bytes to 60,972 for audit evidence fields; exports remain 70.

## 0.233.1 - 2026-08-19

### ➕ Added

- Trusted Rust sessions now expose sequence-zero mutation-job cancellation.
  Cancellation succeeds after a lost response when the record is absent, but
  can remove only a decoded current-format initial Forward record with no
  sequence, receipt, counters, or target effects. Every later logical job must
  use a fresh single-incarnation ID.
- One bounded shared-progress inventory reports the global count and limit,
  reserved integrity headroom, counts by family, and each validated record's
  family, ID, normalized lifecycle, and sequence. Any undecodable retained
  slot fails the entire inventory with `CorruptProgressStore`; raw intent,
  SQL, continuations, values, keys, owners, and replay keys are not exposed.

### 🔧 Changed

- The shared 64-record hard limit now reserves exactly eight slots for Deep
  integrity work. Mutation and generic resumable insertion reject when the
  pre-insert count is 56 or greater; integrity insertion remains eligible
  while the count is below 64. Existing records are not evicted, and retained
  exact-start replay is resolved before new-record capacity admission.
- Cancellation validation and removal share one progress-store borrow. A prior
  batch-policy identity remains cancellable when all zero-effect facts hold,
  because cancellation executes no page policy.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native proofs cover the full cancellation state matrix, current initial
  continuation validation, prior-policy cancellation, three-family inventory,
  fail-closed corrupt-slot handling, and exact admission at 55/56/63/64.
- PocketIC proves lost-response cancellation retry, fresh-ID incarnation
  safety, delayed old cancellation, nonzero-sequence rejection, retained replay
  at the reservation threshold, and all eight integrity-reserved slots.
- Focused warning-denied core, facade, integration, and audit-canister checks
  pass. Repository-wide validation remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Exact cancellation costs 144,089 instructions and an absent retry costs
  37,249. Complete inventory costs 116,690 instructions with one record and
  5,869,961 at all 64 slots, below the 7,000,000 review ceiling.
- Stable progress allocation is 38,993,920 bytes at both 55 and 56 records and
  43,319,296 bytes at both 63 and 64 mixed-family records.
- Relative to the pushed `0.233.0` Patch 2 artifact, compiler Wasm grows by 669
  bytes to 5,024,024 and final raw Wasm grows by 730 bytes to 4,388,468
  (`+0.0166%`). Deterministic gzip shrinks by 53 bytes to 1,700,813. Candid
  remains 60,847 bytes and production exports remain 70; the audit wrappers
  are test-admin-only.

## 0.233.0 - 2026-08-19

### 🩹 Fixed

- Durable mutation-job Forward pages now retain the start-time timestamp only
  for canonical statement identity and use one current advance-message
  timestamp for all writer-managed `updated_at` values in the committed page.
  A matching row written after job start therefore converges instead of
  deterministically retrying an obsolete managed timestamp.
- A target row whose managed time is later than the current message is detected
  during read-only preparation and commits terminal
  `RestartRequired(ManagedTimestampRegression)` progress without target-row
  effects.
- Forward and Verify now stop before a fixed 16 MiB raw scan-byte boundary.
  Forward additionally consumes the structural writer's exact 16 MiB
  key/before/after staging authority, so wide matching and nonmatching rows
  make bounded checkpoint-safe progress instead of selecting an unbounded or
  deterministically failing page.
- A candidate excluded by staging is revisited on the next advance. A valid
  candidate that cannot fit even an empty current page instead commits terminal
  `RestartRequired(CandidateExceedsBatchPolicy)` without target-row effects.

### 🔧 Changed

- Managed timestamp monotonicity remains owned by the structural writer; the
  mutation-job scan reuses that predicate instead of duplicating its rules.
- The timestamp-policy component now participates with the new semantics in
  the mutation-job batch-policy identity. A job retained across a later 0.233
  policy change terminalizes through `BatchPolicyChanged` and must be restarted.
- The final policy identity now also binds distinct Forward/Verify key and scan
  limits, the writer-owned staged-byte limit, and checkpoint-safe prefix rules.

### ⚠️ Breaking

- The public `MutationJobRestartReason` variant set gains
  `ManagedTimestampRegression` and `CandidateExceedsBatchPolicy`. These are
  pre-1.0 exhaustive-match and Candid variant changes; no compatibility alias
  or predecessor protocol is retained.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native owner tests cover exact managed-time monotonicity, current policy
  drift, terminal-record round trips, and terminal acknowledgement.
- A multi-message PocketIC proof starts a job, advances time, performs a later
  matching write, replays the first committed page exactly, and reaches stable
  completion across subsequent messages.
- Exact accounting tests freeze scan and writer-staging boundaries, maximum-row
  admission, and the transient row-buffer envelope. PocketIC wide-row tests
  prove nonmatching scan stops, staging exclusion and revisit, exact replay,
  eventual completion, and maximum accepted index fanout.
- Focused warning-denied core, integration, and audit-canister checks pass.
  Repository-wide validation remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Against released `v0.232.1`, compiler Wasm grows by 12 bytes to 5,019,058,
  while final raw Wasm shrinks by 1,407 bytes to 4,382,571. Deterministic gzip
  grows by 536 bytes to 1,700,312.
- Candid grows by 100 bytes to 60,740 solely for the new restart variant; the
  final artifact retains 70 exports. Persisted allocation and record shapes
  are unchanged; the existing version-1 restart-reason encoding adds one
  current tag.
- The focused 56-row Forward sample peaks at 28,549,314 instructions under the
  unchanged 31,000,000 ceiling. Exact predecessor resampling was unavailable
  after the isolated PocketIC server failed to bind, so no instruction delta
  is claimed.
- After byte packing, the focused 56-row Forward peak is 31,905,351

  7,462,037 under its unchanged 8,000,000 ceiling.
- Relative to the Patch 1 artifact, compiler Wasm grows by 4,297 bytes to
  5,023,355 and final raw Wasm grows by 5,167 bytes to 4,387,738. Deterministic
  gzip grows by 554 bytes to 1,700,866; Candid grows by 107 bytes to 60,847;
