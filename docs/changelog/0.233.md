# 0.233 Durable Mutation-Job Liveness And Recovery

## 0.233.1 - 2026-08-19

### ➕ Added

- Trusted Rust sessions now expose sequence-zero mutation-job cancellation.
  Cancellation succeeds after a lost response when the record is absent, but
  can remove only a decoded current-format initial Forward record with no
  sequence, receipt, counters, or target effects. Every later logical job must
  use a fresh single-incarnation ID.
- One bounded shared-progress inventory reports the global count and limit,
  reserved integrity headroom, counts by family, and each validated record's
  family, ID, normalized lifecycle, and sequence. Any undecodable retained
  slot fails the entire inventory with `CorruptProgressStore`; raw intent,
  SQL, continuations, values, keys, owners, and replay keys are not exposed.

### 🔧 Changed

- The shared 64-record hard limit now reserves exactly eight slots for Deep
  integrity work. Mutation and generic resumable insertion reject when the
  pre-insert count is 56 or greater; integrity insertion remains eligible
  while the count is below 64. Existing records are not evicted, and retained
  exact-start replay is resolved before new-record capacity admission.
- Cancellation validation and removal share one progress-store borrow. A prior
  batch-policy identity remains cancellable when all zero-effect facts hold,
  because cancellation executes no page policy.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native proofs cover the full cancellation state matrix, current initial
  continuation validation, prior-policy cancellation, three-family inventory,
  fail-closed corrupt-slot handling, and exact admission at 55/56/63/64.
- PocketIC proves lost-response cancellation retry, fresh-ID incarnation
  safety, delayed old cancellation, nonzero-sequence rejection, retained replay
  at the reservation threshold, and all eight integrity-reserved slots.
- Focused warning-denied core, facade, integration, and audit-canister checks
  pass. Repository-wide validation remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Exact cancellation costs 144,089 instructions and an absent retry costs
  37,249. Complete inventory costs 116,690 instructions with one record and
  5,869,961 at all 64 slots, below the 7,000,000 review ceiling.
- Stable progress allocation is 38,993,920 bytes at both 55 and 56 records and
  43,319,296 bytes at both 63 and 64 mixed-family records.
- Relative to the pushed `0.233.0` Patch 2 artifact, compiler Wasm grows by 669
  bytes to 5,024,024 and final raw Wasm grows by 730 bytes to 4,388,468
  (`+0.0166%`). Deterministic gzip shrinks by 53 bytes to 1,700,813. Candid
  remains 60,847 bytes and production exports remain 70; the audit wrappers
  are test-admin-only.

## 0.233.0 - 2026-08-19

### 🩹 Fixed

- Durable mutation-job Forward pages now retain the start-time timestamp only
  for canonical statement identity and use one current advance-message
  timestamp for all writer-managed `updated_at` values in the committed page.
  A matching row written after job start therefore converges instead of
  deterministically retrying an obsolete managed timestamp.
- A target row whose managed time is later than the current message is detected
  during read-only preparation and commits terminal
  `RestartRequired(ManagedTimestampRegression)` progress without target-row
  effects.
- Forward and Verify now stop before a fixed 16 MiB raw scan-byte boundary.
  Forward additionally consumes the structural writer's exact 16 MiB
  key/before/after staging authority, so wide matching and nonmatching rows
  make bounded checkpoint-safe progress instead of selecting an unbounded or
  deterministically failing page.
- A candidate excluded by staging is revisited on the next advance. A valid
  candidate that cannot fit even an empty current page instead commits terminal
  `RestartRequired(CandidateExceedsBatchPolicy)` without target-row effects.

### 🔧 Changed

- Managed timestamp monotonicity remains owned by the structural writer; the
  mutation-job scan reuses that predicate instead of duplicating its rules.
- The timestamp-policy component now participates with the new semantics in
  the mutation-job batch-policy identity. Patches 1 through 4 of this line are
  review landings, not deployment-compatible release points.
- The final policy identity now also binds distinct Forward/Verify key and scan
  limits, the writer-owned staged-byte limit, and checkpoint-safe prefix rules.

### ⚠️ Breaking

- The public `MutationJobRestartReason` variant set gains
  `ManagedTimestampRegression` and `CandidateExceedsBatchPolicy`. These are
  pre-1.0 exhaustive-match and Candid variant changes; no compatibility alias
  or predecessor protocol is retained.

### 🧪 Testing

- Native owner tests cover exact managed-time monotonicity, current policy
  drift, terminal-record round trips, and terminal acknowledgement.
- A multi-message PocketIC proof starts a job, advances time, performs a later
  matching write, replays the first committed page exactly, and reaches stable
  completion across subsequent messages.
- Exact accounting tests freeze scan and writer-staging boundaries, maximum-row
  admission, and the transient row-buffer envelope. PocketIC wide-row tests
  prove nonmatching scan stops, staging exclusion and revisit, exact replay,
  eventual completion, and maximum accepted index fanout.
- Focused warning-denied core, integration, and audit-canister checks pass.
  Repository-wide validation remains user-owned.

### 📊 Audit

- Against released `v0.232.1`, compiler Wasm grows by 12 bytes to 5,019,058,
  while final raw Wasm shrinks by 1,407 bytes to 4,382,571. Deterministic gzip
  grows by 536 bytes to 1,700,312.
- Candid grows by 100 bytes to 60,740 solely for the new restart variant; the
  final artifact retains 70 exports. Persisted allocation and record shapes
  are unchanged; the existing version-1 restart-reason encoding adds one
  current tag.
- The focused 56-row Forward sample peaks at 28,549,314 instructions under the
  unchanged 31,000,000 ceiling. Exact predecessor resampling was unavailable
  after the isolated PocketIC server failed to bind, so no instruction delta
  is claimed.
- After byte packing, the focused 56-row Forward peak is 31,905,351
  instructions under a recalibrated 34,000,000 ceiling; stable Verify peaks at
  7,462,037 under its unchanged 8,000,000 ceiling.
- Relative to the Patch 1 artifact, compiler Wasm grows by 4,297 bytes to
  5,023,355 and final raw Wasm grows by 5,167 bytes to 4,387,738. Deterministic
  gzip grows by 554 bytes to 1,700,866; Candid grows by 107 bytes to 60,847;
  exports remain 70.
